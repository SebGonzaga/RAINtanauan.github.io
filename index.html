What RAIN monitors

Five systems, one operations picture

Everything a resident needs before, during, and after a hazard event — built around official sources, not guesswork.

Weather Monitoring

Live conditions, a 6-day forecast, temperature trend chart, and an interactive map — sourced from OpenWeatherMap with your location or any city you search.

Volcano Monitoring

Taal Volcano conditions and a reference alert level, with a link to the official PHIVOLCS advisory for the current level.

AI Virtual Assistance

Ask RAIN in English or Filipino for calm, practical guidance on earthquakes, typhoons, floods, and volcanic eruptions — never a replacement for emergency services.

Emergency Information

One-tap emergency numbers and an evacuation-center map with distance from your location, so help is never more than a glance away.

Disaster Alerts

Verified alerts pushed by LGU administrators, ranked by severity from Information to Critical, with the affected area always stated clearly.

Incident Reporting

Residents can report ashfall, flooding, ground cracks, and other disaster-related incidents directly to disaster response officials, with photo and location.

Real-time transparency

Every reading shows its source and timestamp

RAIN never presents information as live if it isn't. Weather data is sourced from OpenWeatherMap, the volcanic alert level is reference information (not a live feed), evacuation centers and emergency contacts come from the Tanauan City CDRRMO, and any development or demo data is clearly labeled — so residents and responders alike know exactly what they're looking at.

  • Source and "Last Updated" shown on every live component
  • Clear DEMO DATA tags for development-stage content
  • Offline mode shows the last known information, never fabricated updates
