If you need to evacuate

  • Head to the nearest city/municipal hall, school, or covered court — these are standard designated evacuation sites.
  • Bring your go-bag: water, medicines, ID, cash, flashlight, power bank.
  • Turn off electricity and gas at the source before leaving home.
  • Avoid flooded roads, riverbanks, and low-lying areas.
  • Let a family member or neighbor know where you're headed.
